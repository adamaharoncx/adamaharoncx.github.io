**Effective date:** March 18, 2026

WishSync (“we,” “our,” or “us”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how WishSync collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our mobile application.

## Information We Collect

When you use WishSync, we may collect the following types of information:

### Account Information
- Name
- Email address
- Profile details you choose to provide

### User Content
- Wishlist items
- Notes
- Links
- Photos
- Important dates
- Gift plans
- Shared ideas
- Preferences, sizes, favorites, and other information you choose to save in the app

### Relationship and Sharing Data
- Information related to connecting with your partner in the app
- Shared wishlist and date-planning content between connected users

### Device and Technical Information
- Device type
- Operating system
- App version
- Basic diagnostic and usage data
- Push notification token, if notifications are enabled

## How We Use Information

We use your information to:

- Provide and operate WishSync
- Save and sync your content across devices
- Enable partner connection and shared features
- Send reminders and notifications you choose to receive
- Improve app performance, reliability, and user experience
- Protect the security and integrity of the app

## Photos, Links, and Saved Content

WishSync allows users to save content such as photos, notes, links, gift ideas, and important dates. This content is stored to provide the app’s core functionality and is visible only according to the sharing features you choose to use inside the app.

## Notifications

If you enable notifications, WishSync may send reminders and app-related alerts. You can disable notifications at any time in your device settings.

## How Information Is Shared

We do not sell your personal information.

We may share information only in the following limited situations:

- With your connected partner, as part of the app’s intended sharing features
- With service providers that help us operate the app and infrastructure
- If required by law, legal process, or to protect rights, safety, and security

## Data Storage and Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures to protect your information. However, no system is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as necessary to provide the app and fulfill the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, unless a longer retention period is required by law.

## Your Choices

You may be able to:

- Update or delete certain profile and app content within WishSync
- Disable notifications in your device settings
- Request account deletion by contacting us

## Children’s Privacy

WishSync is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

## Third-Party Services

WishSync may rely on third-party services to support hosting, storage, analytics, notifications, or authentication. Those services may process information on our behalf in accordance with their own privacy practices.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will update the effective date above. Continued use of WishSync after changes means you accept the revised policy.
